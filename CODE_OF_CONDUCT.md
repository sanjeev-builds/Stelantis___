# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as team members pledge to make participation in this hackathon project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the team and the project
- Showing empathy towards other team members, especially under hackathon time pressure

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Dismissing or belittling a teammate's contribution or experience level
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, team chat, in-person hackathon workspace) and when representing the team at the event.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to any team member. All complaints will be reviewed and addressed promptly and fairly.
